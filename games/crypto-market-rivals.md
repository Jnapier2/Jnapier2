# Crypto Market Rivals

**Make a decision, see its consequences, and try another approach.**

Crypto Market Rivals is a single-player trading game built around simulated funds, replayable market scenarios, and persistent career progression. Players can explore generated markets, practice at their own pace, revisit historical price references, or build a run around changing conditions in Wild Mode.

[Download the Windows game](https://zappytap.itch.io/crypto-market-rivals) · [Steam listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/5115590/) · In development · Proprietary source

The current itch.io listing calls the unsigned Windows download **Read the Tape**. Its [November 9 release note](https://zappytap.itch.io/crypto-market-rivals/devlog/1764969/read-the-tape-candles-wicks-volume) shows how to read candle ranges, wicks, and simulated volume.

Optional races against fictional rivals add a clear comparison without changing campaign rewards. Session Review and the Field Journal make earlier decisions easier to revisit. Reading, contrast, motion, and sound settings let players adjust the desk, while local checkpoints and backup controls support returning to a career.

The project demonstrates interactive simulation, persistent application state, replay design, and understandable decision feedback. All funds and rewards are simulated and have no cash value; it is a game, not financial advice.

## Try a scenario

Download the Windows game from the release page and follow its setup instructions. Start a practice scenario with simulated funds, make a decision, and open Session Review to inspect its consequences. Try an optional rival race when you want another point of comparison.

Offline play requires no game account. Optional online sign-in and private rooms are experimental and may fail. Public matchmaking, Steam Cloud, and Steam achievements are outside the verified scope of this case study. See the release page for requirements, credits, and content disclosures.
