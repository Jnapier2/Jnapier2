# Workflow & Case Management Platform
+
+Workflow & Case Management Platform turns requests into assigned, time-bound, auditable work. Teams can define a process visually, apply business-calendar service targets, manage queues and approvals, and review the evidence behind each transition.
+
+[Explore the source](https://github.com/Jnapier2/workflow-case-management-platform)
+
+## Highlights
+
+- Visual workflow definitions with versioning and validation before activation.
+- Business-calendar SLA tracking rather than simple elapsed-time estimates.
+- Queue, assignment, approval, dependency, and escalation controls.
+- Governed automation through durable outbox records and reviewable execution state.
+- Dashboards and process insights built from the same case and audit model.
+- SQLite demonstration mode with a documented PostgreSQL deployment path.
