# Workflow & Case Management Platform

**Give every request an owner, a meaningful deadline, and a traceable path forward.**

Workflow & Case Management Platform turns incoming requests into assigned, time-bound, auditable work. Teams can define a process visually, manage queues and approvals, handle dependencies and escalation, and examine the evidence behind each transition.

[Explore the source](https://github.com/Jnapier2/workflow-case-management-platform)

## What it does

The platform brings visual workflow definitions, case assignment, approval steps, service targets, dashboards, and process insights into one experience. Workflow definitions are versioned and validated before activation, so a proposed process can be reviewed before it governs work.

## What makes the design distinctive

Service targets follow business calendars rather than treating every elapsed hour as working time. Dashboards and audit history reflect the same case records, helping reviewers relate a reported status to the events that produced it. Automation remains visible through recorded execution state rather than disappearing behind an unexplained status change.

## Why it is useful

Service and operations teams can use the demonstration to explore clearer ownership, realistic deadlines, and structured exception handling. It shows how a process map can become a usable application while preserving the history needed to review decisions and follow up on delays.
