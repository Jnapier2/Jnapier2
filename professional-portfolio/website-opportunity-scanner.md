# Website Opportunity Scanner

Turn publicly accessible website pages into a practical improvement plan, with page evidence beside each finding.

The Scanner helps a business understand where its message, contact information, or next step may be unclear. Its report organizes priorities, explains why a finding matters, and suggests wording or actions to consider. This gives the reader a path from an observed issue to a decision they can review.

## Explore the demonstration

Inspect the [illustrative report](https://gatewayinformationgroup.com/website-opportunity-scanner#report) to see how findings connect evidence, priority, and suggested next steps. The service page also offers a [free preview](https://gatewayinformationgroup.com/website-opportunity-scanner#preview) for a public website address.

For example, the illustrative report identifies an unclear service area and recommends naming the locations served. That change would help visitors judge whether the business is relevant to them. The example demonstrates the report format; it is not a measured customer result.

## Design choices worth inspecting

- **Keep findings traceable.** Page evidence accompanies a finding so the reader can inspect its basis.
- **Separate observation from interpretation.** Observable checks are distinguished from recommendations that require judgment.
- **Make the next step practical.** Priorities and suggested wording help turn a review into work someone can assess and act on.
- **Respect the site boundary.** The review uses public pages without logging in to or modifying the website.

## Evidence boundary

This is a documentation-only case study with a hosted demonstration; the implementation remains private. On September 29, 2026, the public service page and illustrative report were checked, and 28 focused offline tests passed using synthetic responses. These tests cover evidence prerequisites and bounded page-review behavior. They do not establish live report completion or customer outcomes; live scanning, payment, and report delivery were not exercised in this review.

Findings are recommendations for review, not an accessibility certification, legal review, or guarantee of rankings, sales, or revenue.
