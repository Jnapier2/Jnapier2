Rules you can explain

Region comes from one client lookup. Low spend means spend ≤ threshold: Motion $15,000; Digital $10,000; Print $8,000. These are synthetic demonstration thresholds, not recommendations.

A record is complete only when both hard and soft savings are present. A genuine zero is valid. Incomplete rows remain visible for review but contribute neither savings nor denominator spend to the savings rate. No eligible spend means “Not available,” not a fabricated zero-percent result.
