# Policy and Procedure Navigator
+
+Policy and Procedure Navigator helps people find an applicable policy, understand the supporting evidence, and take the next controlled step. It combines permission-aware retrieval with citations, document status checks, abstention when evidence is insufficient, and structured follow-up workflows.
+
+[Explore the source](https://github.com/Jnapier2/policy-procedure-navigator)
+
+## Highlights
+
+- Evidence-grounded answers with document and section citations.
+- Permission, classification, effective-date, expiration, and draft-status controls.
+- Explicit abstention when the available material cannot support a reliable answer.
+- Review queues and workflow templates for exceptions, clarification, and policy updates.
+- Local demonstration data covering current, expired, draft, and role-restricted material.
+- Diagnostic and release-integrity controls that keep private runtime output out of the repository.
+
