# Policy and Procedure Navigator

**Find applicable guidance—and see the evidence that supports it.**

Policy and Procedure Navigator helps people locate relevant policy material, understand its supporting evidence, and take a controlled next step. It combines cited answers with permission checks, document-status checks, and structured follow-up when the available material cannot support a reliable conclusion.

[Explore the source](https://github.com/Jnapier2/policy-procedure-navigator)

## What it does

Answers link to document and section citations. The navigator considers access permissions, classification, effective dates, expiration, and draft status. Review queues and workflow templates support clarification, exceptions, and policy updates rather than leaving an unanswered question without a route forward.

## What makes the design distinctive

Finding a relevant passage is not treated as sufficient evidence that it applies. The design considers whether the material is current and accessible to the requester, and explicitly withholds unsupported answers. Uncertainty becomes a visible review task instead of being hidden behind confident wording.

## Why it is useful

The project demonstrates a more accountable way to navigate policy collections: make guidance traceable, keep obsolete or restricted material in context, and connect unresolved questions with follow-up. Synthetic examples include current, expired, draft, and role-restricted documents so reviewers can explore these distinctions directly.
